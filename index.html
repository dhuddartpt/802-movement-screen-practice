DPTV 802 • Movement Screen Random Case Practice

Generate two different cases for a pair. Choose one clinically relevant movement, observe, reason, and document.

Before using the randomizer: complete the full movement-menu portion of the student worksheet. Then generate a pair of cases.
  • The clinician reads only the condition and patient report.
  • The clinician looks away while the patient partner reveals and reads the acting cue.
  • Perform one relevant movement, not the entire screen.
  • On the worksheet answer: why this movement, two objective observations, plausible contributors, what to examine next, and ICF.
  • Reveal the debrief only after the attempt is complete.
Practical rule: Relevance matters. A movement screen should be clinically tied to the condition and should produce objective observations that guide the next examination decision.
The two students will always receive different cases.
